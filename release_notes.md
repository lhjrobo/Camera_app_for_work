# WorkCameraApp v2.4.0

## New Features
- **Variable FPS Control**: Choose between Auto (Low Light Optimized), 30fps, or 60fps.
- **Photo Priority Mode**: New "Instant Record" toggle. Turn OFF to maximize photo brightness (matches Open Camera quality) by disabling the video stream in photo mode.

## Improvements
- **Native Resolution**: Fixed resolution drop in Auto mode.
- **Brightness**: Optimized default FPS range to [15, 30] for better low light performance.
